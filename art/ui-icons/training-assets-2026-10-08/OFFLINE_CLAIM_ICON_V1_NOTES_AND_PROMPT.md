Match a bright gold/yellow coin with warm orange-gold side rim, saturated lime/emerald sprout, clean white upper-edge glint, and green winged-shoe game icon style. Crisp, chunky and uncomplicated rather than photorealistic.
Composition/framing: one balanced compact isolated group centered in a square. Complete coins, clock and one white glint fill about 90% of the square, tight crop with slim transparent safety margins. Strong silhouette readable at 32 pixels; avoid fine engraving, dense sparkles or thin clock hands.
Text: NONE. No 2x, multiplier, words, letters, numbers, price, dollar sign or Robux logo. The game draws all labels separately.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely fully transparent alpha behind and around all objects. No black, white, checkerboard or coloured background, no studded plate, no square tile, UI frame, ground shadow, scene or background glow.
Output: one square transparent PNG icon intended for a 512x512 export. One asset only, no icon sheet, mockup or multiple variants.
