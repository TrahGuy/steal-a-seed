**The jaw is open and roaring.** `RoaringJaw` + `MawCore` — there is a lit core
inside the mouth. Mouth depth is a named requirement: it is a real recessed
cavity built from brow, cheek and jaw masses, never a dark panel pasted on.

**The crown is above and behind the skull**, a gold flare with two spikes and a
white-hot core, with three cyan orbital arcs and one small comet satellite
circling it. Those arcs are the Starbloom identity — not optional decoration.

**Six petals** — three inner, three outer — at the base, around a lotus stem.

## Pose

Low, prowling, weight forward, head turned toward the viewer mid-roar. The gold
crown and its arcs above the shoulder line. This is the trophy card in the
collection and it should read as a predator that happens to be a plant.

Crop the rearmost legs and the tail end of the carapace at the frame edge if
needed; the skull, the open jaw, the brow horn, the crown flare and at least
four of the six legs stay in frame.

## Scene

A Starbloom alien garden at night. Luminous flora — bell-shaped and fern-like,
glowing pale cyan and violet from within — clustered low and out of focus.
Celestial formations behind: a nebula sky in violet and deep blue, one bright
core echoing the crown, drifting rock islands catching rim light.

Light the creature from ABOVE AND BEHIND with the crown's own gold, and fill
the shadow side with the scene's cyan. That two-source lighting is what keeps a
near-black creature readable against a dark sky — do not brighten the body to
solve it, and do not put a bright sky directly behind the head.
