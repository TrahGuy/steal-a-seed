# Game contracts and budgets

What the builders and their consumers require of a creature, surveyed from the source on
2026-09-24 (file names given; line numbers drift). Re-read the files before relying on a detail,
and never rename or remove a load-bearing name without updating every consumer and asking first.

## Plants and pods

**Builders** (`src/ReplicatedStorage/SeedGame/Shared/CreatureModel.luau`, required by the server
and by clients such as HatchFX, GardenUI, IndexUI, LoadoutUI and OfflineUI):

- `CreatureModel.Build(species, tier, stage, cf, parent)` sends pods to `BuildPod` and everything
  else to `BuildCreature`. `cf` is always the **BASE** -- the ground contact point, never the centre.
- Every grown plant gets a part named **`Base`** (axis-aligned Block, 0.06 tall, transparency 1) as
  its **PrimaryPart**; `finish()` then adds the `Creature` tag. PlantFormsSpec checks: Base is the
  PrimaryPart at the ground; the lowest drawn point is within 0.03 of the ground; height equals the
  tier's frame height within 1.5%; every part is Anchored with CanCollide, CanQuery and CanTouch
  off; the model is flat -- parts are direct children, no sub-models, scripts, lights or emitters
  for the eight authored species.
- Pods: the PrimaryPart is **`Shell`** at the pod's centre; the builder never tags a pod, callers do.
- Species tables are PartSpecs replayed by `replayPart`; four are generated by `tools/art` tools
  and say so in their headers ([Route A](route-a-primitive-production.md)).
- Stamped attributes: `SpeciesId`, `Rarity`, `Tier`, `Stage`; PlantService adds `PlacementId`,
  `PlantedAt`, `HatchAt`, `Ready`, `Hatching`, `Wander*` and the `Planted` tag. There is no `Kg`
  attribute; weight is the tier.

**Size tiers** (`SeedData.Tiers`): frame height = species `Height` x `mul`.

| tier | Tiny | Big | Huge | Mega | Giant | Titan | Colossal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| mul | 1.00 | 1.38 | 1.90 | 2.65 | 3.70 | 5.10 | 7.60 |
| girth | 0.82 | 0.90 | 0.98 | 1.07 | 1.17 | 1.30 | 1.45 |
| pod diameter | 1.66 | 2.59 | 3.66 | 4.87 | 6.09 | 7.94 | 9.04 |

**Consumers:**

- **PlantSway** (client): follows the `Planted` tag; one `PivotTo` per plant, round-robin at 20 Hz;
  no Motor6Ds, it writes CFrames on anchored parts. Rigs come from **direct children only**: arms
  are parts named exactly `Leaf`; legs are names containing Thigh, Femur, Haunch, Shin, Tibia,
  Knee, Hock, Foot, Hoof, Paw, Toe, Stilt or Pillar (the leg rig needs a Thigh, Femur or Haunch);
  the root walker uses `Root<Word><n>` / `PropRoot<n>` with a `RootRise<n>` hinge (slots 1-4) and
  wins over legs; gaze uses the suffixes `Pupil`, `Eye`, `Glint`, lids `Lid`.
- **PlantGlow** toggles only `Light` instances by world phase; **PlantAura** (Colossals) never
  attaches to parts named Eye, Glint, Cheek, Smile, Lid, Lash, Pupil, Base, Label, and so on.
- **HatchFX** builds the creature on the client, strips it and scales it with `Model:ScaleTo` --
  it needs a PrimaryPart.
- **CarryService**: a carried pod is welded to its PrimaryPart; a held plant's PrimaryPart is
  renamed `Handle` and its reach comes from its extents (`GripReach`). No PrimaryPart, no Tool.
- **Placement**: the ghost disc is a formula (pod diameter, or frame height x 0.46 x girth x 1.5),
  not a measurement of the model; picking tests the bounding box plus 2 studs.
- **Streaming**: planted models are `ModelStreamingMode.PersistentPerPlayer` for their owner --
  part-by-part streaming once left parts 4.6-65.4 studs from their walked plants.
- **UI framing**: `UIKit.framePlant` fits the drawn parts (transparency below 1) at a 38-degree
  field of view looking along `(0.66, 0.28, -1)`, so it assumes the plant faces -Z; the hotbar
  preview pivots the model to the origin, relying on the Base pivot.

## Guardians (nest parents)

- `ParentModel.Build(cf, height, biomeId, parent)` dispatches Dustbowl to Brambleback, Tanglemire to
  Miremaw, Emberroot to Forgemaw, Starbloom to Astralmaw; NestService builds at height 15.
- `HumanoidRootPart` is the PrimaryPart and must **not** be massless.
- Seven Motor6Ds: `RootJoint` (root to Torso, socket at the model origin), `Neck`, `JawJoint`,
  `LeftShoulder`, `RightShoulder`, `LeftHip`, `RightHip`. ParentAnim writes `Motor6D.C0` against a
  captured rest pose and will not register a guardian until `Neck` exists.
- The `NestParent` tag, and attributes such as `BiomeId`, `Species`, `Asleep`, `SleepBodyPose`,
  `RigSeams`, `MeasuredPartCount`; only the Torso collides.

## Specs that guard these contracts

`PlantFormsSpec`, `StarbloomLimbSpec`, `DustbowlPodSpec`, `PlantStreamingSpec` and the guardian
specs. Run the relevant ones after any Route A change; a Route B change needs their expectations
changed too, which is part of what the owner approves.

## Budgets: derive them, never invent them

There is no universal safe number. Measured and recorded so far:

| subject | measured | source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| authored plant species | budget 48 parts (leg species), 56 (root species) | PlantFormsSpec |
| Starbloom plants | 30-81 parts; 1-3 lights; 0-2 emitters | StarbloomLimbSpec |
| Suncrown, Pyrelotus | 63-64 parts (Pyrelotus: 1 light, 1 emitter) | forms tables, PlantFormsSpec |
| guardians | Brambleback 83, Miremaw 73, Astralmaw 83, Greenhollow brute 86 | builders, handoff |
| map | 3,237 parts | server log, handoff |
| importer | 20,000 triangles per mesh ceiling (121,500 became 19,999) | handoff 2026-09-11 |
| one plot, 4 Colossals | 60 fps, client p99 19.0 ms, desktop Studio | handoff |

Capacity: 6 plots per server; plot tiers hold 5 / 7 / 10 / 15 / 20 plants (`GameConfig.PlotTiers`),
so up to 120 planted models per server -- derived, never measured. Five guardians live at once.

To set a budget for a new creature:

1. State the simultaneous count: copies per garden x gardens, or one guardian per biome.
2. Measure the representative scene as it is (parts, triangles, lights, emitters; Studio's Stats
   and MicroProfiler; frame time).
3. Add the candidate at that count, measure again, on desktop Studio **and** a phone or the device
   emulator -- a desktop frame time, and anything measured in Blender, says nothing about a phone.
4. Set the budget with headroom, and record the measurement and the number in the handoff.
5. Ask before exceeding it.

Superseded records not to cite: "480 parts" for a bed of twelve, the Greenhollow-era 25-40 band,
Dustbowl's 31-74 of 2026-08, Suncrown at 76 parts, CreatureModel's old "~1,200 parts" estimate.
