STEAL A SEED -- SOUND PROMPTS FOR SUNO
======================================

Every prompt below is a COPY-PASTE LINE for Suno's Style box. Nothing else on
the line is meant to be typed in.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO RUN THESE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Custom mode. Instrumental ON. Leave the lyrics box EMPTY.
  2. Paste one Style line. Do not add your own adjectives on top -- Suno gets
     literal-minded past about 15 words and starts writing a song instead.
  3. Generate 2 clips, keep the better one, Download -> WAV.
  4. Trim in Audacity: select the part you want, Ctrl+T, then Effect ->
     Fade Out on the last 30ms so it does not click when it stops.
  5. Upload at create.roblox.com -> Creations -> Audio. Anything under six
     seconds clears moderation in about a minute; music takes longer.
  6. Put the id it gives you in GameConfig -- Music.Tracks for a bed,
     GameConfig.Sfx for a one-shot. Do NOT leave the id only in a chat log.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
READ THIS BEFORE YOU DO THE POP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SUNO IS A SONG MODEL. It will not hand you a 90-millisecond pop. It hands you a
clip half a minute long and you cut the pop out of it. That works, but it is
fishing -- so the one-shot prompts below are all written to make Suno produce a
SPARSE clip of REPEATED single hits with silence between them, which gives you
ten or fifteen candidates out of one generation to choose from.

If a one-shot fights you for more than two generations, stop. ElevenLabs Sound
Effects is built for exactly this and is free at this volume, and the Roblox
library already has usable UI clicks. The beds in Part 2 are where Suno is
actually the right tool, and that is where the generations are worth spending.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

milliseconds: 1000 ms = 1 second.

  --   ---------------------------------   ---------------   ----------------

===============================================================================
PART 1 -- THE ONE-SHOTS
===============================================================================

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CARD HOVER POP                                    <- the one you asked for
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Plays when the mouse enters a plant card in the Index, the Garden sheet, or a
shop row -- the hover in UIKit already scales the card up and thickens its
stroke, and this is the sound of that. It fires CONSTANTLY, so it has to be
tiny, dry, and completely without a tail. Anything with reverb on it will smear
into itself the moment somebody sweeps the mouse down a grid.

  STYLE:
  short soft mouth pop, dry, no reverb, one hit every two seconds, silence between, no music, no beat

  Target: 60-110ms. Pitch it up 2 to 4 semitones in Audacity if it reads heavy.
  Level in game: 0.18 to 0.22. It sits UNDER everything -- the alarm pings are
  at 0.42 and a hover sound must never compete with them.

  ALTERNATE, if the mouth pop comes out wet or vocal:
  short wooden marimba tap, single note, very short decay, dry, spaced out with silence, no melody

  One more thing: a hover that plays at the same pitch every time turns into a
  woodpecker on a grid. Randomise PlaybackSpeed 0.94-1.06 on each play.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CARD CLICK / CONFIRM
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The pop's louder sibling. Same family, one step firmer, so the two read as one
instrument rather than two decisions.

  STYLE:
  short warm wood block click, single hit, dry, tight decay, spaced with silence, no music

  Target: 90-140ms. Level 0.3.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DENIED / NOT ENOUGH CASH
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two descending notes. Never a buzzer -- a buzzer says "you broke it" when the
truth is "come back with more money".

  STYLE:
  two soft descending marimba notes, minor second apart, dry, short, no backing, silence around it

  Target: 250ms. Level 0.3.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PANEL OPEN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Index and the shop slide in. Give them air moving, not a UI beep.

  STYLE:
  soft paper swoosh with a low wooden thump at the end, single gesture, dry, silence around it, no music

  Target: 300ms. Level 0.28. For PANEL CLOSE, reverse the same file in
  Audacity and cut it 30% shorter -- closing should be quicker than opening.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. BANKED -- A POD MADE IT HOME
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The most important sound in the game after the alarm, and the alert UI
currently fakes it with a pitched-up stock ping. This is the payoff for a whole
run.

  STYLE:
  bright bell arpeggio up, three notes, glockenspiel, warm, short, ending clean, no drums, no bass

  Target: 700ms to 1s. Level 0.5.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. RARITY REVEAL -- ONE PER TIER, FOUR GENERATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The rarer it is, the longer the sound is allowed to take. Generate all four in
one sitting from the same family, so a Legendary reads as a bigger version of a
Common rather than as a different game.

  COMMON:
  single soft kalimba note, warm, dry, short decay, silence around it, no music

  RARE:
  two bright kalimba notes rising, light shimmer tail, dry, no drums

  EPIC:
  four note glockenspiel run rising with soft choir swell underneath, no drums, no bass, short

  LEGENDARY:
  rising glockenspiel and harp flourish with a low bell hit and airy choir, triumphant, no drums

  Lengths 300ms / 500ms / 1.2s / 2.5s. Levels 0.3 / 0.35 / 0.45 / 0.55.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. MILL UPGRADE BOUGHT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mechanical, not magical -- the mill is a machine and the tier 7 one is still a
machine. A clunk that resolves upward.

  STYLE:
  heavy mechanical lever clunk then a bright bell ding, single gesture, dry, no music, silence after

  Target: 900ms. Level 0.4.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. MOUNTING THE BELT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fires when your own mill catches you mid-sprint. Short and physical -- feet
landing on a rubber deck, not a chime. It must not sound like a reward,
because it happens whether you aimed for it or not.

  STYLE:
  soft rubber thud with a short mechanical whir starting, dry, close mic, no music, silence around it

  Target: 400ms. Level 0.3, positional on the belt rather than 2D.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. CASH TICK
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For the cash counter rolling up after a bank -- NOT for the +$N that rises off
every grown plant once a second. If every one of those made a noise the garden
would be unlistenable inside a minute.

  STYLE:
  tiny coin tick, very short, dry, single hit repeated with silence between, no music, no melody

  Target: 50ms. Level 0.15, and play it every third digit rather than every
  digit.


===============================================================================
PART 2 -- THE BEDS AND LOOPS
===============================================================================

This is what Suno is actually for, and these are worth five to ten generations
each.

Ask for the loopable length in the prompt and then still cut it by hand: find a
bar line near the end, cut there, and crossfade 200ms onto the top.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. GARDEN BED, THIRD TAKE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two takes of Garden at First Light are already in and they wrap at about three
and a quarter minutes. A third pushes that past five, which is roughly where a
bed stops being noticed at all. Match the existing two -- same key, same tempo,
one different texture.

  STYLE:
  gentle acoustic cottagecore instrumental, fingerpicked nylon guitar, soft marimba, warm pads, slow, no drums, loopable

  Target: 2 to 3 minutes. Level 0.32, to match the two already playing.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. NIGHT GARDEN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The same garden, later. Only worth doing if there is ever a day/night cycle --
but it is the cheapest way there is to make a world feel like it has time in
it.

  STYLE:
  quiet nocturnal instrumental, soft felt piano, low warm pad, distant crickets, very slow, sparse, no drums, loopable

  Target: 2 to 3 minutes. Level 0.28 -- a night bed at day volume reads as
  louder, not quieter.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. CHASE BED, SECOND TIER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A chase bed is already uploaded. A second, more frantic one gives the chase
somewhere to escalate TO -- when the pod being carried is expensive, or on the
third theft from the same nest.

  STYLE:
  frantic percussive chase music, driving low toms, urgent plucked strings, rising tension, fast, loopable, no vocals

  Target: 60 to 90 seconds. Level 0.4 -- above the garden bed, below the
  stinger.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. SHOP / INDEX UNDERSCORE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Optional, and it might genuinely be worse than silence. If you make it, it has
to duck the garden bed rather than stack on top of it.

  STYLE:
  light music box and celesta loop, curious, gentle, slow, sparse, no drums, loopable

  Target: 45 to 60 seconds. Level 0.22.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. TITLE / LOADING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The one place a big statement is allowed, because the player is not doing
anything yet and has nothing to hear it over.

  STYLE:
  warm orchestral folk theme, nylon guitar melody, strings swell, hopeful, cinematic, no vocals, no drums

  Target: 30 to 45 seconds, faded under the garden bed at spawn.




===============================================================================
PART 3 -- REPLACING THE FIVE THAT WERE DELETED
===============================================================================

Five uploads are gone and their ids are dead. Every one of them has a real
trigger in the game now, so these are not optional extras -- until they are back
the game has a monster that runs at you in silence.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. GRAB -- THE PARENT REACHES YOU
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The instant before the throw. The chase has been building for the whole run home
and this is the sound of it ending badly, so it wants to be ugly and it wants to
be INSTANT -- no wind-up, because the wind-up already happened, it was the
chase.

  STYLE:
  heavy cloth grab thump with a low leathery snatch, single hit, dry, close mic, no music, silence around it

  Target: 200-300ms. Level 0.75, positional on the parent.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
16. THROW -- THE LAUNCH
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fires about a fifth of a second after GRAB, so the two have to sit together: the
grab is the impact and this is what follows it. Weight first, air second -- a
pure whoosh with no body reads as a swing that missed.

  STYLE:
  fast heavy whoosh with a deep impact at the start, single gesture, dry, no music, silence after

  Target: 500-700ms. Level 0.85, positional on the parent.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
17. TAKE POD -- THE HOLD COMPLETES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not the button, the SHELL COMING FREE. The player has held E for the better part
of a second watching a bar fill, and this is the release at the end of it --
which means it has to feel earned, and it has to be organic. A UI click here
would make a nest raid feel like a menu.

  STYLE:
  soft organic pluck, stem snapping free with a low wooden pop, single hit, dry, no music, silence between

  Target: 250-400ms. Level 0.55, positional on the pod.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
18. CHASE BED (LOOP)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Runs for as long as you are carrying, and ducks the garden bed to a third while
it plays. This is the BASE one -- number 12 in Part 2 is the more frantic second
tier to escalate to, and if you only make one, make this.

It has to loop without a seam. Generate long, then cut on a bar line and
crossfade 200ms onto the top.

  STYLE:
  urgent percussive chase loop, running low toms, tense staccato strings, driving, fast, loopable, no vocals, no melody

  Target: 45-90 seconds. Level 0.40.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
19. CHASE FOOTSTEP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The parent's feet, positional, so a player hears WHERE it is rather than just
that it exists. Its walk is deliberately wrong -- the legs are 140 degrees apart
and one is always late -- so an even four-to-the-floor gait would fight the
animation. Ask for the limp.

  STYLE:
  heavy monster footsteps on soil, slow uneven limping gait, deep thuds, dry, close, no music, no melody

  Target: a 2-4 second loop of steps. Level 0.50, positional on the parent.

  If what you get is a SINGLE step rather than a run of them, that is fine and
  arguably better -- set Looped = false on the ChaseStep cue in GameConfig and
  it will be retriggered per stride instead.

PART 4 -- REBIRTH (2026-10-09)
===============================================================================

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
20. REBIRTH FANFARE                                  <- cue "Rebirth"
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The biggest moment a player can buy: all their cash gone, reborn stronger. It
plays under the rebirth celebration, which is about 3.5 s long:

  0.0 - 1.6 s   the character spins twice, light orbs spiral up   = THE BUILD
  1.6 s         the spin stops, a gold ring bursts out            = THE HIT
  1.6 - 3.0 s   leaves and sparkles drift up, the popup sits      = THE TAIL

So the sound is a RISE, ONE BIG BRIGHT HIT, then a sparkling fade. Nearby
players hear it too, from where the player stands.

It must NOT sound like the Legendary reveal (glockenspiel + harp + choir): a
rebirth is bigger and different. Here the brass carries the hit, and the
rising whoosh carries the build. No drum kit, no beat, no melody that wants
to keep going.

  STYLE:
  magical rising shimmer whoosh building into one bright orchestral brass and bell chord hit, sparkling chime tail, triumphant, no drums, no vocals

  ALTERNATE, if the first one comes out as a song or too epic-movie:
  short reverse cymbal swell into a single sunny brass fanfare chord with harp glissando and twinkling chimes fading out, cheerful, no drums

  ALTERNATE, softer and more "garden" if the brass is too loud:
  rising wind chimes and airy shimmer swell into a warm bell and marimba chord, bright sparkle fade, magical, uplifting, no drums, no vocals

  Target: 2.5 to 3.0 s in total.
  TRIM SO THE BIG HIT LANDS ABOUT 1.5 s AFTER THE START (the build before it
  can be 1.2 to 1.6 s). Cut everything after the sparkle fades, then fade out
  the last 50 ms. If the build you get is shorter, keep it: the game can start
  the sound later so the hit still meets the burst.
  Level in game: about 0.6. It is louder than a reveal and quieter than the
  alarm. Claude measures the WAV and sets the real volume before wiring it.

  Upload as AUDIO and send the id. Until then the game borrows the Legendary
  reveal (RevealLegendary).


===============================================================================
PART 5 -- POD RUSH (2026-10-09)
===============================================================================

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
21. POD RUSH BED (LOOP)                              <- the rush's own music
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Plays for the 5 minutes of a Pod Rush, in place of the normal garden music,
the same way the rain event swaps its own bed in. Everyone on the server hears
it, so it IS the announcement that something is happening. Players are
sprinting, stealing and being chased the whole time, so it should feel urgent
and fun, never scary. The chase music still comes in over it when a guardian
is after you, so it must not compete with that.

  STYLE:
  upbeat playful chase music loop, fast bouncy marimba and pizzicato strings, punchy drums, whistle hook, cheerful urgency, garden game, instrumental, no vocals

  ALTERNATE, if it comes out too frantic or too orchestral:
  energetic cartoon garden race music, plucky ukulele and xylophone, bright snare groove, playful brass stabs, fun and fast, instrumental, no vocals

  Target: 60 to 90 s that LOOPS cleanly. Cut at a bar line where the end
  flows back into the start; no intro build and no ending. About 140 to 160
  BPM. Level in game: about the normal bed's 0.32; Claude measures it first.

  Upload as AUDIO and send the id.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
22. POD RUSH START AND FINISH STINGS (OPTIONAL)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  START (when POD RUSH! is announced):
  short exciting race start fanfare, quick drum roll into bright brass hit, cheerful, 2 seconds, no vocals

  FINISH (when the rush ends and the end card shows):
  short referee whistle then a bright brass ta-da chord, fun, 2 seconds, no vocals

  Target: about 2 s each, trimmed tight, a 50 ms fade at the end.

===============================================================================
WHERE THE IDS GO
===============================================================================

One table, one line each, in
src/ReplicatedStorage/SeedGame/Shared/GameConfig.luau:

    GameConfig.Sfx.Cues = {
        CardHover  = { id = "", volume = 0.20, ... },
        CardClick  = { id = "", ... },
        ...
    }

Paste the id between the quotes and that cue starts playing. An EMPTY id is a
silent cue, not a broken one -- nothing errors, nothing warns twice, and the
game runs exactly as it does today until an id arrives. So they can go in one at
a time, in any order, and be heard immediately.

Music beds go in GameConfig.Music.Tracks instead -- that is a plain list, so a
third garden take needs no code at all.
